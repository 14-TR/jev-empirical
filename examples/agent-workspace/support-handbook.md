# Support handbook — returns quick reference

Original authored fictional fixture, MIT licensed. No real customer data.

Last substantive revision: 2025-11-15. Based on Return Policy v2.

Tell customers that unopened stationery can be returned within 30 days of
purchase with a receipt. No restocking fee applies to eligible returns.
The handbook has not been reconciled against the later policy change register.
Its continued presence in the workspace does not establish current authority.

Example from 2025: a customer asking on day 20 was inside the published return
window. Check the applicable policy version before applying that example to a
newer purchase.
