# Return policy v3 — Harbor Paper Shop

Original authored fictional fixture, MIT licensed. No real customer data.

Owner: Customer Operations. Approved policy text, version 3.

Unopened stationery may be returned within 14 days of purchase with proof of
purchase. There is no restocking fee for an eligible return. A return outside the
applicable window requires an explicit discretionary exception; support staff
must not promise one automatically.

The effective date, transition rule, and authority over earlier guidance are
recorded in change-register.md. Do not infer an effective date from this file's
name or filesystem timestamps. The support handbook may describe a different
window, so consult the approved register when the sources conflict.
